ClipGrabber

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ClipGrabber (“the app”) is a desktop application that runs on your computer. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your data stays on your computer, and the app sends it only to the services you choose to use.

1. Data we collect

We, the developers, do not collect any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own that receive your data.

2. Data stored on your computer

  • Downloaded videos, subtitles, the clip library and settings, in folders on your computer.
  • Login tokens for Twitch, YouTube and TikTok, in the Windows Credential Manager. Passwords are never seen or stored by the app: you log in on the service's own page.
  • A local log file for troubleshooting. It does not contain tokens.

You can delete this data at any time by logging out in the app, deleting the files or uninstalling the app.

3. TikTok

If you choose to share a video to TikTok, you log in with TikTok and the app requests these permissions:

  • user.info.basic — your display name and avatar, shown in the app so you can see which account you are posting to;
  • video.upload — to send a video you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draft;
  • video.publish — to post a video you selected, with the caption and privacy settings you chose, after you press “Post”.

The app sends to TikTok only the video file you selected and the settings you entered. It does not read your other videos, followers, messages or any other TikTok data. The TikTok access token is stored only on your computer and is deleted when you log out. You can also revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services.

4. Other services

  • Twitch — clip search and download through the official Twitch API.
  • YouTube — if you choose to upload a video, the app uses the YouTube Data API with the permission to upload videos only. Use of YouTube data follows the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram — the app only copies the file to the clipboard and opens Telegram Desktop; it does not connect to Telegram itself.

Data you send to these services is handled under their own privacy policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. We cannot see it.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the services it connects to in your country).

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
