ClipGrabber

ClipGrabber is a free desktop app for Windows. It helps streamers and clip editors find the most popular Twitch clips of a channel, download them in bulk, add word-by-word subtitles and share the finished videos.

What it does

  • Search Twitch clips by channel, period and views (official Twitch API).
  • Download many clips at once into a folder on your computer.
  • Generate subtitles on your computer with a speech-recognition model; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Keep a library of downloaded and watched clips.
  • Share a video from the “On device” tab: upload it to YouTube, send it to your TikTok account (as a draft or as a post), or copy it to Telegram.

TikTok integration

When you choose “Share → TikTok”, ClipGrabber asks you to log in with TikTok and grant access. You can then either send the video to your TikTok inbox to finish editing in the TikTok app, or fill in the caption and privacy settings in ClipGrabber and post it directly. A video is only uploaded after you press the button yourself. You can log out at any time, and the app forgets your TikTok token.
